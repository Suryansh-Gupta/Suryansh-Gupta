# Market Size, Pricing, Unit Economics and Funding: AI "Autopilot" Accounting Manager (as of Nov 2026)

Note on sources: most market-size figures come from paid research reports, and only their public summary pages were seen. Several figures reached me through search-result summaries rather than full-page reads, and are marked "(search summary)". Treat every research-firm number as directional.

## 1. Market size and CAGR (accounting software, accounting/bookkeeping services, outsourced bookkeeping, AI in accounting, financial close)

### Takeaway
The labor (services) budget dwarfs the software budget. US accounting services are about $155B (IBISWorld, 2025) and US payroll and bookkeeping services about $76.5B. Global accounting software is only about $20–22B, growing roughly 9–10% a year. The "AI in accounting" category is small, but research firms forecast 25–40% CAGRs, and their estimates vary about 3x on the base year.

## 2. Pricing models and competitor price points

### Takeaway
There are three pricing tiers: DIY ledger software at $0–340 per month (QuickBooks, Xero, Puzzle, Digits, Kick); bookkeeping-as-a-service at about $200–600+ per month, flat or tiered by expense volume (Pilot, Zeni, Bench); and enterprise AI-native ERP/close tools that are sales-led with unpublished prices (Rillet, Campfire, Numeric). Outcome-based and agent pricing is a thesis promoted by VCs, but few SMB price points are public.

### Cited Findings
- QuickBooks Online US list prices from Aug 1, 2026: Simple Start $38/mo (1 user), Essentials $85 (3 users), Plus $140 (5 users), Advanced $340 (25 users). Previous prices were Essentials $75, Plus $115 and Advanced $275, so the 2026 increases were about 13–24%. — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/small-business/business-software/how-much-does-quickbooks-cost); [QuickBooks price-change blog](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/product-update/quickbooks-price-changes/) (search summary)
- Pilot: G2 lists pricing from $99 up to $499 per month. Pilot's own blog cites bookkeeping at $199/mo (Essentials) and $599/mo (Core). The two sources conflict. — [G2 Pilot pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/pilot/pricing); [Pilot blog](https://pilot.com/blog/digits-alternatives) (search summary)
- Zeni: G2 lists the Full Service plan from $549/mo. A Pilot blog cites $299/mo for Essential, which looks dated. — [G2 Zeni pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/zeni/pricing); [Pilot blog](https://pilot.com/blog/best-zeni-alternatives) (search summary)
- Digits: about $30–65/mo, depending on the source. Puzzle: free Starter tier, with paid plans around $25–50/mo. Kick: free to $40/mo, with the $40 Basic plan billed annually ($480). All three figures come from third-party aggregators. — [Jupid](https://jupid.com/blog/best-ai-bookkeeping-tools-2026); [AI Accounting pricing comparison](https://ai-accounting-global.pages.dev/pricing-comparison/); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/guides/best-ai-bookkeeping-tools) (search summary)
- Bench paused operations in Dec 2024, was acquired by Employer.com within days, and resumed service in Jan 2025. — [BestAIAccounting](https://bestaiaccounting.com/compare/pilot-vs-bench/) (search summary)
- Xero's blended ARPU is about NZ$55/mo, a proxy for what the SMB DIY tier pays on average. — [Rask Media](https://www.raskmedia.com.au/2026/05/14/xero-asxxro-share-price-on-watch-after-31-growth-in-fy26-result/)
- a16z (Dec 2024): AI is driving a shift toward outcome-based pricing. Back-office finance administration, previously done by human labor, can now be automated and packaged as software. — [a16z enterprise newsletter](https://a16z.com/newsletter/december-2024-enterprise-newsletter-ai-is-driving-a-shift-towards-outcome-based-pricing/)

### Inferences
- A plausible autopilot price band is about $150–500/mo: above QBO Plus ($140), and at or below human-in-the-loop BaaS such as Pilot or Zeni ($199–599+). Tiering by monthly expense volume or transaction count matches how BaaS vendors price.
- Per-task or outcome pricing, such as per closed month or per reconciled account, could differentiate the product, but there are no public SMB benchmarks to validate it.

### Gaps
- Official 2026 pricing pages for Pilot, Zeni, Digits, Puzzle and Kick were not fetched directly. Figures come from aggregators and conflict.
- Rillet, Campfire and Numeric do not publish prices. No ACV data was found.
- No source was found for percentage-of-spend pricing in accounting. That model exists in spend management (e.g., card interchange), not bookkeeping.

## 3. Unit economics benchmarks

### Takeaway
SMB SaaS benchmarks put CAC at about $1.1–1.4K, payback at 9–15 months, and annual gross churn at 11–13% median (ChartMogul) to 20–25% (KeyBanc via secondary source). Pure SaaS gross margins run about 75–85%. AI products averaged about 45% in 2025 (ICONIQ), tech-enabled services about 30–50%, and Pilot about 60% (2022 estimate). Inference cost per customer is not well documented in public sources.

### Cited Findings
- ChartMogul 2025 report (2,200+ companies): median annual gross churn for SMB customers (1–50 employees) is 11–13%, with the top quartile at 7–8%. — [WeAreFounders summary](https://www.wearefounders.uk/saas-churn-rates-and-customer-acquisition-costs-by-industry-2025-data/) (secondary)
- ProfitWell/Paddle 2025: SMB gross revenue churn of 6–10% a year. KeyBanc 2025 (per a secondary source): SMB-focused SaaS at 20–25% annual churn. — [WeAreFounders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/saas-churn-rates-and-customer-acquisition-costs-by-industry-2025-data/); [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026) (secondary, unverified)
- Average B2B SaaS churn of 3.5% a month in 2025 (2.6% voluntary, 0.8% involuntary), about 35% a year. — [Stealth Agents](https://stealthagents.com/research/saas-churn-rate-statistics-2026) (secondary)
- OpenView 2025 (n=519): SMB with ACV of $1K–10K has a median CAC of $1,100–1,400 and payback of 9–15 months. Another source puts SMB payback (under $15K ACV) at 8–12 months, and an outlier cites 18–24 months. — [WeAreFounders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/saas-churn-rates-and-customer-acquisition-costs-by-industry-2025-data/); [Growth Unhinged 2025 SaaS Benchmarks](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-saas-benchmarks-report) (secondary)
- a16z (2020): gross margins for AI companies are about 50–60%, between software's 60–80%+ and services' 30–50%, because of compute and human data curation. — [a16z, The New Business of AI](https://a16z.com/the-new-business-of-ai-and-how-its-different-from-traditional-software/) (older source, 2020)
- a16z "Services-led growth" (June 2025): customization means lower gross margins and higher burn at first, and companies should optimize for growing total gross profit rather than margin early. — [a16z](https://a16z.com/services-led-growth/)
- ICONIQ (via secondary source): average AI-product gross margin of 45% in 2025, projected at 53% in 2026, versus about 80% for SaaS. — [Offset Partners](https://www.offsetpartners.io/resources/ai-startup-gross-margin-benchmarks) (secondary)
- Pilot: Sacra estimated about $43M ARR and a 60% gross margin, which it called industry-leading, as of 2022. This figure is stale. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/pilot-mechanical-bookkeeper/)
- AI-native bookkeeping services are claimed to run at 50–60% gross margin after review and QA, with a path to 80–85%. This is a vendor-modeled claim, not reported data. — [Kanopy Labs](https://kanopylabs.com/blog/ai-native-service-companies-yc-bets-2026)
- Sequoia's "Services: The New Software" thesis: the real money is in capturing the labor budget, not the software budget. — [Linas's Newsletter summary](https://linas.substack.com/p/sequoiathesis)

### Inferences
- At $300/mo ($3,600/yr), a $1,200 CAC and 60% gross margin give a payback of about 6.7 months ($1,200 / ($300 × 0.6)). At a 3% monthly logo churn, customer lifetime is about 33 months, so LTV ≈ $300 × 0.6 × 33 ≈ $5.9K, an LTV/CAC of about 5x. At a 5% monthly churn, LTV/CAC drops to about 3x. These are illustrative calculations, not sourced data.
- LLM inference cost (assumption, not sourced): an SMB with 300–1,000 transactions a month at a few thousand tokens per categorization or reconciliation step is likely to use low single-digit millions of tokens a month. At blended prices of roughly $1–5 per million tokens, that is likely $5–50 per company per month, or about 2–15% of a $300 price. The dominant COGS is likely the human CPA or reviewer time for exceptions and month-end review, not inference. Verify against current model price sheets.

### Gaps
- No primary access to the KeyBanc, Bessemer or ChartMogul reports. Churn and CAC figures come via secondary aggregators and conflict.
- NRR benchmarks for SMB were not found. A common rule of thumb is about 90–100% for SMB, but this is unsourced.
- No reported, measured LLM cost per company for an accounting agent was found.
- Bench's gross margin was never published. Pilot's current margin is unknown.

## 4. Startup costs (MVP, integrations, SOC 2, insurance, CPA reviewers)

### Takeaway
Hard compliance and integration costs are modest: about $25–40K cash for a first SOC 2 Type II, plus Plaid (sales-priced, with a reported $1K/month minimum). Expect the main costs to be engineering and CPA reviewer headcount. No reliable published figures were found for MVP build cost, E&O insurance or CPA salaries.

### Cited Findings
- Vanta: SOC 2 total cost (prep plus audit) commonly runs from $10K to $80K+, and it recurs yearly. — [Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/what-does-a-soc-2-audit-cost)
- Drata: first-year SOC 2 cost ranges from about $25K for a small startup to $200K+ for a large enterprise, with a 25-employee scenario at about $28K. — [Drata](https://drata.com/blog/soc-2-audit-cost)
- Type II audit fee alone: $8–25K. Vanta subscription from about $10K/yr for SOC 2 only. A 50-person example totals about $36.5K for tooling plus audit. — [SOC2Auditors.org](https://soc2auditors.org/insights/vanta-pricing/); [UnderDefense](https://underdefense.com/blog/vanta-pricing/) (search summary)
- Thoropass: total first-year investment of $120–350K once internal staff time is included. — [Thoropass](https://thoropass.com/blog/soc-2-audit-cost-a-guide)
- Plaid: Transactions is subscription-billed per Item per month, even when no API calls are made. There is a free tier with 200 live calls per product, and paid pricing is sales-led. Third-party estimates range from $0.25 to about $1.50 per connection per month. A $1,000/mo business minimum is reported but unconfirmed. — [Plaid billing docs](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/); [Plaid pricing](https://plaid.com/pricing); [Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/plaid-pricing-2026-cost-per-connection-and-request); [Monetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/articles/plaid-vs-yodlee-how-much-will-financial-data-apis-cost-your-fintech)

### Inferences
- With a 4–6 person founding team over 6–12 months, an MVP budget is likely dominated by salaries, roughly $0.5–1.5M. That is consistent with the median $4.1M seed round in section 5. This is an estimate, not sourced.
- At $0.25–1.50 per connection and about 3 bank or card connections per SMB, bank-feed cost is likely under $5 per customer per month at scale. It is not a material COGS driver.

### Gaps
- No source was retrieved for Intuit App Partner Program (QuickBooks API) fees or Xero API/app partner fees.
- No E&O or professional liability insurance quotes for AI bookkeeping firms were found.
- No current CPA or staff accountant salary benchmarks were retrieved. Use BLS or Robert Half data.

## 6. Illustrative TAM/SAM/SOM for a US SMB autopilot product

### Takeaway
On stated assumptions, the bottom-up US TAM is about $20B a year (5.58M employer SMBs at $300/mo). The SAM is about $6.8B a year (about 1.4M firms with 5–499 employees on a cloud ledger, at $400/mo). A 5-year SOM of 0.5% of SAM is about 7K customers and about $34M ARR. The top-down cross-check is about $155B in US accounting services labor spend.

### Cited Findings (inputs)
- 5.58M US firms with 1–499 employees in 2023 (5.53M in 2022), per the Census Business Dynamics Statistics. — [Census Small Business Week 2026](https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/small-business-week.html) (search summary)
- 36.2M total US small businesses (under 500 employees), including nonemployers. 62.3M small business employees. — [SBA Office of Advocacy 2025 profile](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/United_States_2025-State-Profile.pdf)
- Pew Research: about 6M small employer businesses (2021 ABS), 49% of which have 1–4 workers. — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2024/04/22/a-look-at-small-businesses-in-the-us/)
- Price anchors: QBO Plus is $140/mo and Pilot is $199–599/mo (section 2). US accounting services are $154.9B and payroll and bookkeeping $76.5B (section 1).

### Inferences (model; all assumptions are explicit)
| Layer | Assumption | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| TAM | All 5.58M US employer SMBs at $300/mo blended ($3,600/yr) | 5.58M × $3,600 | about $20.1B/yr |
| TAM upside (not included) | About 30.6M nonemployers (36.2M − 5.58M) at $30/mo DIY tier | 30.6M × $360 | about $11B/yr |
| SAM | Firms with 5+ employees (about 51% of employer firms ≈ 2.85M) × 50% on a cloud ledger (QBO or Xero) that the product can integrate with ≈ 1.43M firms, at $400/mo ($4,800/yr) | 1.43M × $4,800 | about $6.8B/yr |
| SOM (year 5) | 0.5% of SAM firms | 7,125 × $4,800 | about $34M ARR |
